## Analysis & Takeaways

1. **~50% Latency Reduction**: SSP-ARR consistently cuts task waiting time by virtually 50% across all 12 workload scales, regardless of which Tier-1 global scheduler is selected.
2. **Global Scheduler Invariance**: Whether using dynamic standard deviation thresholding (DeRALBA) or static priority ordering (PSSLB), local VM time-slicing optimizations dominate the waiting time gains.
