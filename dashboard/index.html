Proposed Algorithm

Each VM independently governs its own local queue. Instead of static round robin slicing, SSP-ARR dynamically decides whether to execute reactively or predictively.

Mode = (CV_j ≤ 0.50) ? Reactive : Predictive
Inputs:
Head task from Q_j, sliding burst history of last W=5 completed cloudlets.
Outputs:
Optimal time slice quantum Q_j, updated task remaining burst.
Parameters:
History Window W = 5 | Bounds: Q_min = 0.001s, Q_max = 60.0s

12-Week Capstone Project Development Logbook

Verified diary entries tracking the research from problem formulation to IEEE publication readiness.

Week 1–2: Literature Survey & Simulation Selection
Problem Formulation & CloudSim Plus 8.5.7 Environment

Surveyed CPU scheduling literature, identified fixed quantum thrashing in Round Robin, selected CloudSim Plus 8.5.7 with Java 21, and extracted real-world Google Cloud Jobs (GoCJ) benchmark traces.

Week 3–4: Infrastructure & Baseline Modeling
30 Hosts, 80 Heterogeneous VMs & Classical RR Baseline

Built the 30-host datacenter topology with SPECpower linear power profiles, modeled 80 heterogeneous VMs (1000–4000 MIPS), and implemented standard Classical Round Robin (q=1.0s) with hardware context switch penalty (δcs = 5ms).

Week 5–6: SSP-ARR Algorithmic Formulation
Coefficient of Variation Volatility Sensor & Dual-Mode Slicing

Designed the mathematical model for SSP-ARR: Coefficient of Variation (CV = σ / μ), Safe Reactive median slicing, Adaptive EWMA forecasting with error-modulated α rates, and Starvation Aging priority bonuses.

Week 7–8: Confidence Gating & Multi-Global Integration
NPE ≤ 20% Gate & 5 Global Datacenter Dispatchers

Formulated Confidence Gating via Normalized Prediction Error (NPE) to prevent drift, and integrated 5 Tier-1 Datacenter Global Schedulers: Dynamic Max-Min, DRALBA, PSSLB, PSSELB, and DeRALBA.

Week 9–10: Comprehensive Benchmarking & Baselines
12 GoCJ Scales (100–2000 Cloudlets) vs RR & MAADRR

Conducted extensive empirical runs across all 12 GoCJ trace scales, measuring AWT, ATAT, ACT, Makespan, Throughput, Energy (kWh), and Preemptions against Classical RR and MAADRR. Demonstrated consistent 50.3% AWT reduction and 99.3% preemption elimination.

Week 11–12: IEEE Paper Publication & Observability Platform
IEEE Conference Paper, High-Res Figures & Dashboard Deployment

Authored full 2-column IEEE Conference Paper, generated 10 high-resolution publication charts, and built the production observability dashboard deployed to Vercel and Render for live external examination.
